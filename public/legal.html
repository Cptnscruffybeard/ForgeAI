Legal & privacy framework

This MVP includes a framework, not legal advice. Before public launch, have counsel review the final terms for the jurisdictions and data practices you actually operate under.

Privacy

ForgeAI should collect only information needed to provide diagnosis, recommendations, account functions and product improvement. Do not submit sensitive personal information, regulated records, passwords, API keys or customer secrets into the diagnosis form. Production deployment should use encryption in transit and at rest, managed authentication, access controls, retention limits, backups and an incident-response process.

Recommendations

Recommendations are informational. Tool facts are presented with verification and source metadata. When a fact cannot be verified, the production system should mark it unknown rather than inventing it.

Affiliate disclosure

If ForgeAI receives a commission from a vendor, that relationship should be disclosed clearly and near the recommendation or link. Affiliate compensation must not silently change the recommendation criteria.

Third-party data

ForgeAI must not copy proprietary directories, reviews or databases without permission. External discovery should be limited to sources with an applicable license, API agreement, vendor permission or other lawful reuse basis.

Security

The MVP applies defense-in-depth controls including strict headers, CSP, input limits, validation, rate limiting and protected administrative ingestion. Production deployment requires additional controls such as managed identity, MFA/passkeys, WAF, centralized logging, secret management, security testing and database access controls.
